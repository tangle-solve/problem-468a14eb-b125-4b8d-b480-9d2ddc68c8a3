### CLIM-000009: Convective self-aggregation in the real tropical atmosphere
- **Subfield:** Convection and the hydrological cycle
- **Open:** Determine when spontaneous aggregation in idealized radiative-convective equilibrium controls observed tropical organization. Predict dependence on temperature, rotation, domain size, shear, and surface coupling.
- **Status:** Studied since the 1990s; RCEMIP reproduced aggregation across many models in 2020 but exposed substantial disagreement under standardized conditions.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Connects mesoscale storms with humidity, radiation, and precipitation extremes.
- **Key works:** Wing et al. (2020), [“Clouds and Convective Self-Aggregation in a Multimodel Ensemble of Radiative-Convective Equilibrium Simulations”](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020MS002138).
- **Related:** Nonequilibrium pattern formation; phase transitions in driven systems.
